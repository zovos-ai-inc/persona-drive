Single sign-on and sessions

Lumen supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect on the Team and Business plans.

Session length

An agent session lasts 12 hours from sign-in, or 8 hours when your identity provider sets a shorter limit. Administrators can shorten it to as little as 1 hour under Settings → Security.

Setting it up

Paste your identity provider metadata URL under Settings → Security → Single sign-on and assign the Lumen application to your agents.
