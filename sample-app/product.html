Product

A queue, a knowledge base and reports, built for teams of two to fifty.

Shared inbox

Email, chat and a web form land in one queue. Assign, set a due date, and let the SLA timer do the nagging.

Knowledge base

When a ticket closes, Lumen drafts an article from it. Nothing goes live until someone on your team reads it.

Reports

First-response time, backlog age, and CSAT, by agent and by team, exportable as CSV.
